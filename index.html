About the Studio

Last Sumerian is a Dubai-based independent cinematic studio founded in 2025 by filmmaker and visual artist Meysam Deris. We create original films, science fiction series, and immersive storytelling experiences.

With 15+ years in:

  • visual arts
  • photography
  • and filmmaking

we combine human vision with AI to build cinematic worlds without limits.

AI is not our storyteller. It is the instrument that lets us tell stories once impossible to produce independently.
